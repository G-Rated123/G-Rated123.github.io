About TriDecaTron:

What is TriDecaTron?

TriDecaTron is a fun and simple word game. Players can use the three letter wheels to spell words which match the image shown above.

How to play?

  • Find out what the image is showing
  • Use the up and down arrows on the wheels to spell out the word
  • Press the 'check' button to find out if your answer was correct

What is the point?

TriDecaTron's aim is to both provide education for those learning to write in English or simply provide an entertaining experience
